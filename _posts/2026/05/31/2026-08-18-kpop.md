I have only listened to Kpop recently, but I have to admit that I love it a lot. Before, I always wonder why Vietnamese music doesn't get into the global chart, because in my personal opinion, Vietnamese music is AWESOME. But now that I listened to Kpop, I can see the stark contrast and how far Vietnamese music needs to go in order to reach the top. 

One of my favourite Kpop groups is Stray Kids (SKZ), which a boy band consists of 8 members, with our amazing leader Bang Chan. They had released a new album recently, and it's called 'This & That', so check it out. They have a lot of good songs, so I can't really say I have a favourite one. Personally, I really love 'Holiday', 'Run It', 'After You', 'This & That', 'Maniac', 'Case 143', and TO BE CONTINUED. 

I think Stray Kids make a lot of meaningful music, and I love hearing their lyrics. It's not just the melody that makes me like their songs, it's also the dance, the lyrics and the meaning. 

Stray Kids also have shows you could watch like 'SKZ Code', and more and more and more. And it is very funny, and in my personal opinion, I love seeing Korean gameshows because it gives very natural vibes. It makes me feel like everyone is playing and actually enjoying themselves without all the pressure of acting. This makes it less funny compare to Vietnamese gameshows, but personally, I love Korean gameshows. 

(If you're looking for gameshows to watch, we have the Masked Singer, and Running Man. Fun fact: Vietnam also have the Masked Singer and Running Man, which they take from Korea.)

I think Kpop Idols are super cool. They have to sing AND dancer at the same time, and their choreography/dance break were NEVER easy. Go see 'BAD' by ATEEZ. Go see the dance break. 

I hear a lot of people saying that Kpop Idols can't do this, they can't do that (This & That mentioned!), but personally, Kpop Idols can already do a bunch of things.

They can sing, they can rap, they can dance. They can even do fast flows, and their body control is just perfect (have you see ITZY? ATTEZ? BTS? Seventeen?).

Back to Stray Kids for a little bit. Out of all the Kpop Idols I have seen, my favourite it Kim Seungmin from Stray Kids. I think he gives very cute vibes, and he could look cool when he wanted too. His vocals are also AMAZING (have you heard him cover Birds of a Feather by Billie Eilish? Have you heard his voice in Case 143?), and I really love the way he talks and interacts with other members. I also really like Hyunjin and Felix, also from Stray Kids. I love all of Stray Kids, every single one of them have an amazing talent. 

I get into Kpop because of [Hyosang](https://linktr.ee/hyosang), [Billy Han](https://www.youtube.com/@BillyHan), and my friends (who are obsessed with Kpop). Honestly, I don't regret getting into Kpop. The music is beautiful, the people are beautiful, the love from the fans are beautiful, the concerts are also beautiful. 

I had made some changes ever since I listen to Kpop. There is this one episode by [Allure](https://www.youtube.com/@Allure) where the Stray Kids members were there, and there was this one question for I.N which asked if he had used any of the other members' cleansing products. And Felix did mentioned that Changbin has very nice skin. And so after that, whenever I feel like I don't want to wash my face, I keep telling myself that I 'would get pretty skin like Changbin', and suddenly I'll get the motivation to do it. 

Listening to their music makes me very happy and lightens my mood. Looking at their edits and watching SKZ code also makes me smiles a lot. Thank you so much, Stray Kids!
